    The Four Factions

    One broken Union, four claims on its flag. Read more about The Divided States

    New England

    Cut off from the capital when Washington fell, the loyalist Federal government retreated to New England — the last stronghold still flying the old flag, hemmed in by the Revolutionary States on every landward side.

    See the faction intro on YouTube ↗ Buy the faction propaganda poster ↗

    American Union State

    Huey Long's American Union State, the “Blue States,” claims the legitimacy of the 1936 election and leans on German arms to push back the Red advance. With Washington retaken, it now moves to cut the Revolutionary States off from the sea.

    See the faction intro on YouTube ↗ Buy the faction propaganda poster ↗

    Revolutionary States

    Born of the Chicago Uprising with Russian and French backing, the Revolutionary States' R.S.A. governs the Great Lakes as a federation of labor unions under a “Third Pillar” Chairman — the Combined Syndicates of America, to give them their full name.

    See the faction intro on YouTube ↗ Buy the faction propaganda poster ↗

    Congressional States

    Fled from Langdon's crackdown, the Congressional States are the remnant of Congress and anti-Langdon governors holding a tenuous Rocky Mountains ceasefire — the smallest of the factions, but backed by Japan and Royalist Canada.
